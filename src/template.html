Key insights

What our team learned at each show. Full notes are in Notion under Grade10 / Card Shows.

ASSC Card Show, Macau · 10 Oct 2026

  • Sports cards dominate. Almost every vendor is sports-led; only a few also carry Pokémon. High-end sports inventory is the easiest gap to fill for a Grade10 show.
  • Strong appetite for the 2027 Grade10 show. AceCards (Taiwan) and CherryCollectibles (Australia) raised 2027 timing; Cardboard Corner (Philippines) is eager to come.
  • Thailand has competing shows. CardWorld BKK (16–18 Oct) is large, draws many Macau vendors and invited Grade10 to take part. Cardster runs a smaller one.
  • Overseas sellers want the HK market. RelikksCard (Canada) and MBA (US) are actively exploring Hong Kong, and several vendors are open to G10 consignment.
  • Mainland vendors face friction. PenceCards struggles to bring inventory across for tax reasons, so expect cross-border logistics to be a barrier.
  • Partnerships beyond booths: MBA (grading partner for the G10 store), AMCC (Panini supply), Frozon (protective card cases, OEM/ODM).

HK Trading Card Show, Asia Expo · 26–27 Sep 2026

  • HK inventory is monotone. Mostly Japanese and modern; high-end English and graded vintage chases (Gold Star, Shining, Level X) are almost absent. Heatpvck and Kenshin are routes to fill the gap.
  • Consignment pipeline for the G10 store: StrayCollective PH, Kenshin and SenditSaturdays all want to consign. Due diligence first.
  • Alt.xyz (US auction house with a Delaware vault) is very warm on the Feb show and store plans.
  • Unserved collectors: Slab-Z sees a hidden base of vintage One Piece and Dragon Ball collectors in HK; trade nights or trade tables at the G10 show could reach them.
  • Vendor risk: an Oripa booth drew bad publicity on Threads. Vet private-collector vendors carefully.
